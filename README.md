# On the energy and momentum evolution in collisionless dark matter flow

Xu Z., 2026, *On the energy and momentum evolution in collisionless dark matter flow*, Zenodo [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24094701](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24094701); the earlier version is hosted on arXiv as [arXiv:2202.04054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.04054).

## Major findings

- The cosmic energy equation dE/dt + H(2K − nW) = 0 holds to within 4.0 per cent at all redshifts z ≤ 5 in an Einstein–de Sitter simulation of 256³ particles.
- The kinetic energy grows as a<sup>1.23</sup> for z ≤ 1, not as a<sup>3/2</sup> (K ∝ t); with the local exponent 1.17 at z = 0 the energy equation predicts n<sub>e</sub> = −1.369, and the simulation gives −1.365.
- For haloes of 100 to 300 particles the constants α<sub>s</sub>* = σ<sub>v</sub>²r<sub>g</sub>/(Gm<sub>h</sub>) ≈ 0.240 and β<sub>s</sub>* = Hr<sub>g</sub>/σ<sub>v</sub> ≈ 0.106 are nearly independent of mass, α<sub>s</sub>* is also independent of time, and β<sub>s</sub>* ∝ a<sup>−0.28</sup>.
- The spin parameter falls from 0.271 for halo pairs to 0.037 for haloes of more than 1000 particles.
- The radial momentum of spheres follows linear perturbation theory, G<sub>V</sub> = −(aH/3)δ<sub>V</sub>⟨|x|²⟩ (correlation 0.83 to 0.97), and their angular momentum grows at second order, ∝ a<sup>3/2</sup>.
- The whole-box radial momentum of a periodic simulation depends on where the box is cut, so its value carries no physical information.
- The integrals I<sub>2</sub> and I<sub>4</sub> of the velocity correlation do not converge for this spectrum.
